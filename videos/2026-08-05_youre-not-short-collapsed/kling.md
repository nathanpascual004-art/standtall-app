# Kling — image-to-video animation prompts

**Do NOT run yet.** These animate the still frames from `frames.md` once they're
generated and approved. Image-to-video, 9:16, ~1080p, one clip per frame.
Motion is subtle and anatomical — the glass figure performs the real movement while
the **red active muscle pulses**. Keep the neon environment and character (`@image1`)
identical to the source still. Slow, controlled; no morphing of the skeleton.

**Clip length target:** 2–3s each (trim to 1–2s in montage for the fast cuts).

## HOOK (from Frame 1A, pre-tuck slump)
Slow push-in on the slumped glass figure; head slightly jutting forward, faint red
strain flicker in the neck. Camera drifts in ~10%. Cyan haze drifts. 2s.

## STEP 1 — CHIN TUCK
- **1A →** Chin draws straight back over 1.5s, gaze stays level, red neck flexors
  brighten as forward-head ghost retracts. Gentle. Cyan.
- **1B →** Side profile: chin tucks back, cervical vertebrae stack, red glow blooms.
  Micro camera orbit ~5°. Cyan.
- **1C →** Low hero: subtle upward camera rise, chin retracts, red flexors pulse once.

## STEP 2 — WALL ANGELS (Y–W)
- **2A →** Arms slide Y→W→Y one smooth rep, elbows draw down/in, red traps+rhomboids
  brighten on the down-phase. Lower back stays glued to wall. Violet.
- **2B →** ¾ view: scapulae retract and depress with the slide, red glow tracks the
  contraction. Slight parallax drift. Violet.
- **2C →** Macro: single scapula glides, red rhomboid fibers pulse with the slide.
  Shallow DOF breathing. Violet.

## STEP 3 — DEAD HANG
- **3A →** Figure hangs; spine visibly lengthens/decompresses over 2s, red spine +
  lats glow travels downward. Very slight sway. Sun-gold.
- **3B →** Side profile: vertebral spaces open as it decompresses, red spine pulses,
  faint dust floats up. Sun-gold.
- **3C →** Low hero up the body: camera eases up the lengthening spine, red glow
  intensifies toward the top. Sun-gold.

## CTA (from CTA frame)
Camera slow pull-back on the fully aligned figure; skeleton settles from any residual
red to calm white; posture holds tall and open. Pink/rose. 2–3s. End on a held,
stable frame with headroom for the CTA text.
